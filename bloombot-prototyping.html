Prototyping

I built these tools for my hands-on courses in interactive electronics and advanced prototyping. Graph paper, lattice structures, encoder wheels, and a 3D model viewer: useful objects for students learning to make things.

I 341 · I 549 Physical Computing

Student Tools

3D Graph Paper
An isometric grid generator for sketching three-dimensional forms by hand. Configure line spacing, color, and page size, then print it and draw on it.
I 341 · Generator→
Lattice
A parametric lattice structure generator. Adjust node count, spacing, and connection patterns to produce printable diagrams for physical structure work.
I 341 · Generator→
Encoder Wheel
A rotary encoder wheel designer. Configure slot count and geometry, then print the wheel for physical computing projects with optical or Hall effect sensors.
I 341 · Physical Computing→
Gear Generator
An involute spur gear designer with two meshing gears. Adjust teeth, module, diametral pitch, and pressure angle, then export SVG or DXF for laser cutting.
I 341 · Generator→
Encoder Design
An extended encoder design tool with additional parameter controls. It's part of the encoder design sequence in advanced physical computing coursework.
